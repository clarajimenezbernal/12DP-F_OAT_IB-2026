### **PROJECT NAME:**

Disease project: Ornithine Aminotransferase Deficiency 

### **GENERAL OBJECTIVE:**

Comprehend Ornithine Aminotransferase deficiency from a genetic, biological, and molecular point of view, such that we are able to understand its causes, the changes it produces and the consequences on the biochemical processes and phenotypes of affected individuals.

### **SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES:**

1. Define what Orthorine Aminotransferase is, including its functions, characteristics, localization, structure, and sequence.
2. Compare the sequence alignment of wild-type OAT with the mutant variants, analysing the causes of the differences and the consequences of the protein structure and functions. 
3. Compare the homologs of OAT found in humans with two other non-human species and identify the similarities and variations.
4. Learn how to use the software platform GitHub and familiarize ourselves with the websites.
5. Optimize the use of a Gantt chart in order to guarantee an efficient work flow and equitable weight of responsibilities for each member of the group.

### **MILESTONES:**

M1: Project scope, overview and objectives defined
M2: Create an updated and completed database research
M3: Establish the analysis and interpretation of OAT and its variants
M4: Final project revised and completed
