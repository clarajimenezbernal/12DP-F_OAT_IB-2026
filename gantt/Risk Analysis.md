**JUSTIFICATION**

1. Our first identified risk is having difficulties comprehending how to use the platforms needed to complete our project. We have realised that, to complete our work, we have to be able to use new software platforms such as GitHub, PubMed, and others that are not easy to operate. Our contingency plan consists of spending some of our work time learning how to use these platforms and searching for online tutorials. If we were to encounter more difficulties, we would ask colleagues or professors for assistance.

2. Furthermore, it is possible that we are unable to find sufficient information about our studied enzyme homolog in other non-human species. We noticed this because one of our mandatory species is quite unknown to us, and we could not find a large amount of information on our first search. In order to prevent this, we will consult a variety of databases and scientific papers, not only depending on one.

3. Another problem we identified is all the outdated information from the databases and research papers. This could seriously affect our project because some details may no longer be up-to-date. We have realised this because a lot of the reviews and research papers were published more than a decade ago, with information that has been further studied and changed. Our contingency plan is to make sure to check the publication dates and prioritise those which are more recent.  
     
4. Finally, the last problem we identified was the possibility of one of our work members being delayed in their assigned tasks. We have realised this because we are still getting used to university schedules, and we may have to prioritise other responsibilities. Our contingency plan is to first make sure this does not happen, since our workload is dependent on our predecessor, but in case it unfortunately happens, we would redistribute this task amongst the team members so it can be finished quicker.
